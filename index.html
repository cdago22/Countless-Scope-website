Core Technical Pillars

Comprehensive Engineering & Field Support

From initial concept & detailed instrumentation sizing to site commissioning and QA/QC verification, we provide full support across the project lifecycle.

PILLAR 01

Engineering & Design

Robust technical design and detailed engineering packages tailored for process control, safety instrumented systems (SIS), and power distribution.

  • PLC, DCS, and SCADA System Architecture Design
  • Instrument Index, Sizing & Specification Datasheets
  • Single-Line Diagrams (SLD) & Electrical Load Calculations
  • Control Panel Layouts & Interconnection Wiring Diagrams
PILLAR 02

Project Supervision & Management

Experienced site engineering leadership ensuring contractor compliance, safety adherence, schedule tracking, and technical integrity.

  • On-Site C&I and Electrical Resident Engineering
  • Vendor Management & Technical Query (TQ) Resolution
  • Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) & Site Witnessing
  • Progress Tracking, Risk Mitigation & Safety Oversight
PILLAR 03

Construction & Installation

High-precision physical installation support for field instruments, cabling, containment, control panels, and power switchgear.

  • Field Instrument Mounting, Tubing & Bench Calibration
  • Cable Tray Routing, Pulling & Gland Termination
  • HV/LV Switchgear & MCC Panel Installation
  • Instrument Loop Checking & Cold Continuity Testing
PILLAR 04

QA/QC & Commissioning

Rigorous quality assurance audits, international standard compliance verification, and systematic commissioning execution.

  • Inspection Test Plans (ITP) & Quality Management Audits
  • Regulatory & Standard Compliance (IEC, IEEE, ISA, NEC)
